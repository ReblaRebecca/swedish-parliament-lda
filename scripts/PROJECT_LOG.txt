Swedish Parliamentary Motions on Education: LDA Analysis
Project Log
===========================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Tracing thematic change in Swedish parliamentary motions on education (1986-2025) using temporally binned LDA with one-to-one topic alignment.

DATA PREPARATION (COMPLETED)
----------------------------
• Downloaded Swedish parliamentary motions from Riksdagens Öppna Data
• Filtered for education committee (UbU) motions: 13,917 total
• Organized into 4 temporal bins:
  - 1986-1993: 3,770 motions
  - 1994-2004: 3,313 motions
  - 2005-2014: 3,577 motions
  - 2015-2025: 3,257 motions
• Generated CSV lists of filtered motion filenames and dates:
  - filtered_motions_UbU.csv (full list)
  - filtered_motions_UbU_group1_1986-1993.csv
  - filtered_motions_UbU_group2_1994-2004.csv
  - filtered_motions_UbU_group3_2005-2014.csv
  - filtered_motions_UbU_group4_2015-2025.csv
