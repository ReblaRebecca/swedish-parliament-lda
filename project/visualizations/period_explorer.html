Riksdagen · Utbildningsutskottet (UbU) · Motions, sessions 1985/86–2025/26

Topics across periods

One LDA model per period, topics linked across periods.

Searches only the top words shown in each cloud, also as part of longer words (skola finds gymnasieskola). A word that is not found can still have some probability in the topic; it is just not among the top words.

Rows are topic chains
Each row follows a possible topic through the four periods. Topics in neighbouring periods are matched according to how similar their vocabularies are.
Chain continuity
A chain is considered continuous when its average similarity is at least 0.50. Chains below this value are marked in gold.
Link similarity
The number between two periods shows how similar the matched topics are. A value below 0.50 indicates an uncertain match and is marked in gold. Because every topic must be matched, weak links may connect topics that are not meaningfully related.
Topic share
The percentage shows the topic’s average proportion across all motions in that period. It does not show the percentage of motions assigned to the topic, since every motion can contain several topics. The topic shares within each period add up to 100%.
Changes between the first and last periods are only calculated when every link in the chain reaches 0.50.
Word size
Larger words have a higher probability within the topic. Word sizes can be compared between periods in the same chain, but not between different chains.
Word colour
Colour shows in how many periods the word appears among the topic’s 20 most probable words:
all four periods three periods two periods one period
Stability
The stability label shows how often the topic was found again when the model was rerun four times with different random starting points. Gold indicates that it appeared in fewer than three runs.
High: found in all four runs · Medium: three runs · Low (gold): two or fewer.
