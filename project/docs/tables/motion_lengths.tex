\caption{Motions referred to the Committee on Education (UbU), sessions 1985/86--2025/26, per period (electoral
terms), the motions filtered out before modelling, and the length of the body text of the modelled motions.
\emph{Placeholders}: motions with no text of their own: the document is missing (6), its conversion was never finished
(1) or the motion was withdrawn (7). \emph{Under 20 words}: fewer than 20 words after preprocessing, almost all short
formal requests (\emph{hemställan}) referring to another motion. \emph{Duplicates}: final texts identical to an earlier
motion, mostly the same motion resubmitted in a later session; the first copy is modelled. Words per motion are counted
\emph{before} preprocessing (the extracted body text, without title, motion number and author names) and \emph{after}
it (the final text, lemmatized, without boilerplate and stopwords). A word is a token with at least one letter.}
