\steprow{01 Extraction}{10,360,449}{137,917}
\hline
Data source & Riksdagens öppna data (data.riksdagen.se): nine motion datasets in XML, \texttt{mot-1980-1989} to
  \texttt{mot-2022-2025}, 160,222 files & Open & Unpacked 2026-10-15; the download date is to be confirmed. The datasets
  are updated while a session is running, so the version matters for session 2025/26. & & \\
\hline
Corpus & Motions referred to the Committee on Education (UbU, XML field \texttt{organ}), sessions 1985/86--2025/26 &
  Motivated & 13,957 motions, 13,943 after the placeholders (below). UbU motions start in January 1986 (motions of autumn 1985 are not tagged with the
  committee). & & \\
\hline
Periods & Four periods of complete electoral terms, assigned by parliamentary session (XML field \texttt{rm}) &
  Motivated & As equal in size as the terms allow: 4,029 / 3,477 / 2,879 / 3,572 motions. The session is used rather
  than the date, since some motions carry a placeholder date. & & \\
\hline
Document unit & Whole motion, body text only; the title is kept as a separate field & Motivated & Natural unit of
  meaning. Gordon uses 1,000-word chunks of books. Title weighting (3$\times$, 5$\times$) was tested and dropped. & & \\
\hline
HTML to text & Text pieces joined without separators; whitespace only at block-level tags & Motivated & Fixes a bug:
  the source HTML often splits single words across inline tags (\textit{uppföljni}/\textit{ng}), and joining with spaces
  produced word fragments in 4,431 motions. & & \\
\hline
Character repair & Soft hyphens removed; Unicode NFC; spacing diacritics repaired (\textit{a°} $\to$ \textit{å}) &
  Motivated & Encoding artifacts of the source documents. & & \\
\hline
Title heading & Removed from the body if found within its first 400 characters & Default & The title opens almost every
  body as a heading (about 97\% of motions); the 400-character window has not been tested. & & \\
\hline
Metadata & Motion numbers (\textit{1989/90:Ub742}) and the motion's own author names removed & Motivated & Done before
  Stanza, so numbers are not split into odd tokens and names are not lemmatized. Gordon also filters author and title
  tokens. & & \\
\hline
Placeholders & Skipped: texts under 30 words noting a missing document, an unfinished conversion or a withdrawn
  motion & Motivated & 14 motions (6 missing, 1 unconverted, 7 withdrawn) with no text of their own. & & \\
\hline
Duplicates & 551 identical final texts (3.95\%), mostly the same motion resubmitted in a later session; only the
  first copy is modelled (541 skipped, the rest are under the length minimum) & Motivated & Kept, a resubmitted motion would weight its words several times in the topics and
  count once per session in the shares, mostly in the last period. & & \\
\hline

\steprow{02 Lemmatization}{10,360,449 (0\%)}{96,875 ($-30$\%)}
\hline
Lemmatizer & Stanza, Swedish Talbanken model (tokenization, part of speech, lemma), run on the running text &
  Motivated & Swedish inflection splits one word into many forms (\textit{skola, skolan, skolorna}); each word is
  replaced by its lemma, so no words are removed. Every word is saved with its form, lemma and part of speech, so later
  steps can be re-run without Stanza (about 4 hours). Gordon does not lemmatize (English). & & \\
\hline
%
% ---------------------------------------------------------------- 03
\steprow{03 Boilerplate removal}{8,727,080 ($-16$\%)}{95,950 ($-1$\%)}
\hline
Boilerplate & 460 parliamentary words and phrases (institutions, procedure, references, party names), matched on the
  original word forms & Manual & A word is removed only if a phrase covers the whole word. Removes many words but few
  different ones: a small set of frequent phrases (\texttt{config/boilerplate\_phrases.txt}). & & \\
\hline
Auxiliaries & Dropped (part of speech \texttt{AUX}) & Motivated & Stanza lemmatizes the modal \textit{ska} to
  \textit{skola}, which would merge with the noun \textit{skola} (school). & & \\
\hline
Punctuation & Dropped (part of speech \texttt{PUNCT}, \texttt{SYM}) & Default & Standard practice. & & \\
\hline
Abbreviation lemmas & Dropped when Stanza expands an abbreviation into a multi-word lemma (\textit{t.ex.} $\to$
  \textit{till\_exempel}) & Default & Counted for review; not tested whether any carry content. & & \\
\hline
Lemma corrections & A short list of known Stanza errors (\textit{kompet} $\to$ \textit{kompetens}) & Manual & Corrects
  the errors found in the model review (\texttt{config/lemma\_corrections.txt}); does not replace the lemmatizer. & & \\
\hline
%

\steprow{04 Normalization}{8,421,273 ($-3.5$\%)}{84,337 ($-12$\%)}
\hline
Normalization & Lowercase; digits removed; punctuation inside tokens replaced by a space; single characters dropped &
  Default & Standard practice. Hyphenated lemmas are split (\textit{it-kompetens} $\to$ \textit{it}, \textit{kompetens};
  \textit{1990-talet} $\to$ \textit{talet}). Removes the spelling variants with punctuation that Stanza keeps
  (\textit{bl.a.}, \textit{läs-}). & & \\
\hline
%
% ---------------------------------------------------------------- 05
\steprow{05 Stopword removal}{4,596,721 ($-45$\%)}{84,170 ($-0.2$\%)}
\hline
Stopwords & NLTK Swedish list (114) plus 129 supplementary words, including their lemma forms & Manual & The
  supplementary words were chosen by inspecting early models. Content abbreviations (\textit{fn}, \textit{eg}) are kept.
  Removes almost half of the words but only 167 different ones: stopwords are few and very frequent. Gordon uses a
  178-word stoplist. & & \\
\hline
Proper names & Kept & Open & Gordon removes them (capitalization heuristic). In the first (44-topic) models, about 8
  topics were organized around places (constituency signal); not yet checked for the current models. & & \\
\hline
Minimum length & Motions with fewer than 20 words in the final text are not modelled & Motivated & 278 motions, almost
  all short formal requests (\textit{hemställan}) referring to another motion, 271 of them in 1985/86--1993/94: too short
  for a reliable topic mix. With the duplicates, the models use 13,124 motions. & & \\
\hline
%
% ---------------------------------------------------------------- vocabulary
\steprow{Frequency limits: input to the whole-corpus model}{3,973,669 ($-14$\%)}{15,763 ($-81$\%)}
\hline
Document frequency & Words in $\geq 10$ and $\leq 50\%$ of motions, no cap & Motivated & Gordon's rule (2026,
  \S3.3), used for the period models and the whole-corpus model: 15,763 different words (3,973,669
  words, 87.9\% of the words of the modelled motions' final text). His documents are 1,000-word chunks of books; our motions
  are much shorter (median 174 words after stopword removal), so a word has fewer chances to reach 10 documents. & & \\
\hline
Upper limit & $\leq 50\%$ of motions & Open & Removes 12 words, among them \textit{utbildning} (62\% of motions) and
  \textit{skola} (51\%), central content words of an education corpus. \textbf{Question:} drop them (they do not
  distinguish between topics) or keep them? & & \\
