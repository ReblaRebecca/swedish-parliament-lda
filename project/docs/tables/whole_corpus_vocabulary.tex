  10,000 & 85.7\% &
  The cap was inherited from the first version of the scripts and was never justified or tested. In practice it acts as a
  minimum document frequency of 20: the 10,000th word occurs in 20 motions, so the cap removes 15,499 words that occur in
  5--19 motions. Gordon uses no cap. The period models already follow Gordon's rule (next row) without a cap. \\
\addlinespace
Gordon's rule &
  in $\geq 10$ and $\leq 50\%$ of documents; no cap &
  15,962 & 87.8\% &
  Gordon (2026, \S3.3) keeps tokens that occur in at least 10 and at most 50\% of chunks (following Schofield et al., 2017).
  His documents are chunks of about 1,000 words; his corpus has 14,973 chunks (2,143--5,254 per bin). Our corpus has a
  similar number of documents (13,953 motions; 2,879--4,025 per period), so a minimum of 10 documents is a similar share of
  the corpus, but our documents are much shorter: after preprocessing a motion has a median of 169 words (mean 331);
  20\% have fewer than 100 words and only 6\% reach 1,000. A short document gives a word fewer chances to occur in it, and
  gives the model less co-occurrence evidence per document.
  \textbf{Question:} is a document-frequency rule calibrated on 1,000-word chunks reasonable for short motions, or should
  the minimum be lower (as in the next row), or relative to the number of documents? \\
\addlinespace
Lower minimum, no cap &
  in $\geq 5$ and $\leq 50\%$ of motions; no cap &
  25,499 & 89.5\% &
  Keeps rarer words: 9,537 words that occur in only 5--9 motions are added to Gordon's rule. This may suit short documents
  better, but rare words add many sparsely filled columns and may make topics less stable across seeds; not tested.
  \textbf{Question:} is the gain in coverage (1.7 percentage points over Gordon's rule) worth the larger vocabulary? \\
\midrule
Upper limit (all options) &
  $\leq 50\%$ of documents &
  $-12$ words & &
  The same limit as Gordon. In the whole corpus it removes 12 words that occur in more than half of the motions:
  \textit{förslag} (77\%), \textit{få}, \textit{mening}, \textit{riksdagsbeslut}, \textit{också}, \textit{utbildning} (61\%),
  \textit{möjlighet}, \textit{viktig}, \textit{därför}, \textit{motivering}, \textit{annan} and \textit{skola} (51\%).
  In Gordon's long, thematically varied chunks such a limit mainly removes general words. In our short motions, all about
  education, it also removes \textit{utbildning} and \textit{skola}, central content words of the corpus.
  \textbf{Question:} is it desirable to drop them (they do not distinguish between topics), or should they be kept?
  The same question applies to the period models, where the limit is applied within each period. \\

\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{Corpus and documents}} \\
Corpus & Motions referred to the Committee on Education (UbU, XML field \texttt{organ}), sessions 1985/86--2025/26 &
  Motivated & 13,957 motions. UbU motions start in January 1986 (motions of autumn 1985 are not tagged with the committee). \\
Periods & Four periods of complete electoral terms, assigned by parliamentary session; as equal in size as the terms allow &
  Motivated & No electoral term is split between periods; largest/smallest period = 1.40. Confound: the opposition is
  largely the same parties within periods 2 and 3. \\
Document unit & Whole motion, body text only & Motivated & Natural unit of meaning. Gordon uses 1,000-word chunks;
  our motions are much shorter (median 169 words after preprocessing). Title weighting (3$\times$, 5$\times$) was tested
  and dropped: small stability gain, a free parameter. \\
Duplicates & 515 identical texts (3.69\%), mostly the same motion resubmitted in a later session; kept & Open &
  A resubmitted motion counts once per session, which weights recurring proposals. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{Preprocessing}} \\
Lemmatization & Stanza, Swedish Talbanken model & Motivated & Swedish inflection splits one word into many forms
  (\textit{skola, skolan, skolorna}); reduces the number of different words by 30\%. Gordon does not lemmatize (English). \\
Auxiliaries & Dropped (UPOS \texttt{AUX}) & Motivated & Stanza lemmatizes the modal \textit{ska} to \textit{skola}, which would
  merge with the noun \textit{skola} (school). \\
Title heading & Removed if found within the first 400 characters of the body & Default & The title opens almost every body
  as a heading; the 400-character window has not been tested. \\
Boilerplate & 440 hand-picked parliamentary phrases (institutions, procedure, party names), matched on word forms & Manual &
  Documented and open to review (\texttt{config/boilerplate\_phrases.txt}). \\
Lemma corrections & A short list of known Stanza errors (\textit{kompet} $\to$ \textit{kompetens}) & Manual &
  Corrects the errors found in the model review; does not replace the lemmatizer. \\
Abbreviation lemmas & Dropped when Stanza expands an abbreviation into a multi-word lemma (\textit{t.ex.} $\to$
  \textit{till\_exempel}) & Default & Counted for review; not tested whether any carry content. \\
Normalization & Lowercase; digits, punctuation and single characters removed & Default & Standard practice; hyphenated
  lemmas are split (\textit{it-kompetens} $\to$ \textit{it}, \textit{kompetens}). \\
Stopwords & NLTK Swedish list (114) plus 85 supplementary words & Manual & The supplementary words were chosen by
  inspecting early models; Gordon uses a 178-word stoplist. \\
Proper names & Kept & Open & Gordon removes them. In the first (44-topic) models, about 8 topics were organized around places
  (constituency signal); not yet checked for the current models. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{Vocabulary}} \\
Period models & Own vocabulary per period: words in $\geq 10$ and $\leq 50\%$ of the period's motions, no cap & Gordon &
  4,926--7,981 words per period. Whether a rule calibrated on 1,000-word chunks suits short motions is open
  (Table~\ref{tab:whole-corpus-vocabulary}). \\
Whole-corpus model & Words in $\geq 5$ and $\leq 50\%$ of motions, at most 10,000 words & Default & Inherited, never
  justified; see Table~\ref{tab:whole-corpus-vocabulary}. \\
Upper limit & $\leq 50\%$ of documents & Gordon & Removes \textit{utbildning} and \textit{skola} in the whole corpus;
  whether that is desirable is open (Table~\ref{tab:whole-corpus-vocabulary}). \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{Model}} \\
Algorithm & scikit-learn LDA, online variational Bayes & Motivated (open) & Chosen for practicality; Gordon uses MALLET
  (Gibbs sampling). The study is framed as an adaptation of Gordon's workflow; to be confirmed with the supervisor. \\
Passes & 50 passes, no convergence check & Default & Gordon runs 1,000 Gibbs iterations. Every fit runs exactly 50
  passes; whether the topics have converged is not checked (a 200-pass comparison is planned). \\
Online learning & Mini-batches of 128 motions; learning decay 0.7, offset 10 & Default & scikit-learn defaults; not tested. \\
Priors & Symmetric, $1/k$ for document--topic and topic--word & Default & scikit-learn defaults; Gordon optimizes
  asymmetric priors every 10 iterations. \\
Topics & 8 per period; 32 for the whole corpus & Motivated & 8 is the highest tested count (8, 11, 15) with at least
  half of the topics stable in every period; 32 = 4 $\times$ 8 gives the same total capacity, as Gordon's 60 = 4 $\times$ 15. \\
Seed of reported models & 42 & Default & Arbitrary; seed stability is tested separately. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textbf{Evaluation}} \\
Seed stability & 5 seeds (42, 123, 456, 789, 999), Hungarian matching on cosine & Motivated & Gordon tests seed stability
  only for whole-corpus runs; here every model is tested. \\
Stable topic & Found again (matched cosine $\geq 0.70$) in at least 3 of the 4 other seeds' models & Default & A judgement
  call, stated as such; the threshold has not been varied. \\
Alignment & Adjacent periods, one-to-one Hungarian matching on cosine over shared words & Gordon & Cannot represent a topic
  splitting or merging. \\
Chain criterion & Mean of the three links $\geq 0.50$; single links $< 0.50$ marked weak & Gordon & All 8 chains hold;
  4 have every link $\geq 0.50$. \\
Permutation test & 1,000 random one-to-one matchings per transition; $z$ and one-sided normal $p$ & Gordon &
  $z = 4.2$--$5.6$; not directly comparable with Gordon's $z = 7$--$9$ at 15 topics. \\
Top words & 20 per topic (Jaccard, period explorer), 30 in the topic explorer & Default & Display and comparison
  choice; not tested. \\
