Riksdagen · Utbildningsutskottet (UbU) · Motions 1986–2025

Topics across periods

One LDA model per period, topics linked across periods.

Rows are chains
Each period model has its own topics. Topics of adjacent periods are matched one-to-one (Hungarian algorithm on cosine similarity), and a row follows these matches from the first period to the last.
Link
The cosine similarity between a topic and the matched topic of the previous period.
Share
Mean topic proportion over the period's motions; every motion counts equally.
Seed chip
In how many of the 4 other seeds' models the topic is found again (cosine ≥ 0.70); gold when fewer than 3.
