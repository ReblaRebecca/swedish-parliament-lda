Topics across periods

One LDA model per period, topics linked across periods.

Searches only the top words shown in each cloud, also as part of longer words (skola finds gymnasieskola). A word that is not found can still have some probability in the topic; it is just not among the top words.

Rows are chains
Each period model has its own topics and its own vocabulary. Topics of adjacent periods are matched one-to-one (Hungarian algorithm on cosine similarity over the words both periods' vocabularies contain), and a row follows these matches from the first period to the last.
Chain holds
Gordon's criterion: the mean cosine of the chain's three links is at least 0.50.
Link
The cosine similarity between a topic and the matched topic of the previous period, over the words both periods' vocabularies contain.
Share
Mean topic proportion over the period's motions; every motion counts equally. Rise and fall (first vs last period) are computed only for chains with every link at or above the threshold, which is stricter than “holds”: a change across a weak link may compare unrelated topics. Broken chains are listed after the others.
Word size
The word's probability in that period's topic, on one scale per chain.
Word colour
In how many of the chain's four periods the word is among the top words.
all 4 3 2 1 period
Seed chip
In how many of the 4 other seeds' models the topic is found again (cosine ≥ 0.70); gold when fewer than 3.
